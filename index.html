1. The weekend is a split market

Between Saturday 17:32 and Sunday 19:46 UTC, 0 of 411 closed tokens changed price, while 75 of the 77 live ones did. The frozen group is exact, not approximate.

A finding about the API itself. On the tokens I checked by hand (NFLX, KLAC, PPLT, ACN), the token list's tokenPrice equals referencePrice × tokenToShareRatio. Comparing them naively shows fake gaps of about 900%. Separately, /rwa/price, adjusted for the share ratio, shows exactly zero premium on all 488 tokens in every priced snapshot. The two prices behave as one number, so there is no on-chain-vs-reference gap to trade.

2. Two issuers, two weekend prices

Sunday 19:46 UTC. Ondo price vs bStock price for the same stock. Ondo is higher in 21 of 24 pairs, which suggests a steady difference between the feeds. SKHY runs the other way.

Read this before trusting it.
  • One weekend only, 53 stocks, and stocks move together, so these are not 53 independent tests.
  • The target is the Tuesday Oct 6, 17:58 UTC price, about 28 hours after Monday's open, because a power cut stopped me recording Monday. It is not the opening print.
  • The Friday baseline was taken 46 minutes after the close, not at it.
  • ETHA jumped 204% (20.07 to 61.04) and is excluded as a probable data fault.
  • Weekend moves correlate with the later move at about 0.46: some information, not an edge.

How it was built

Node.js scripts sign requests to /api/v1/dex/market/rwa/tokens and /rwa/price, snapshot all 488 tokens, then compare snapshots: recorder.js, compare.js, live.js, pairs.js, grade.js.
